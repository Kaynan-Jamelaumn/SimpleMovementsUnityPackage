# 05 — Knowledge, Instruments and Skills

[← Agriculture](README.md)

The player is never told what to do. There is no "needs nitrogen", no "too acidic", no hint of the right depth, no
icon over a thirsty plant: understanding comes from **observation, experimentation, instruments, experience and
deduction** — the player reaches the conclusion on their own.

## What a player is told

**Look** (and every action's answer) describes only **what is seen**, in words:

* the soil: its colour and feel ("Dark, crumbly soil that smells of the forest floor, moist."), what lies on top, a hole
  ("a fingertip deep"), whether it was worked;
* a plant: what it is and how long ago it was sown, how big it is, its stage in plain words, its **symptoms** — pale
  old leaves, purple leaves, brown leaf edges, yellow between the veins, pale new leaves, dead tips, burnt leaves,
  wilting, waterlogged leaves, frost-bitten leaves, sun-scald, leggy growth — the pests on it, spots and mould, dead
  wood, its produce piece by piece ("On it: three small, young fruits, one ripe fruit.").

Never the cause (which lack makes the leaves pale), never a number (unless an instrument read it), never the remedy.
The same symptom can have several causes — pale old leaves may be hunger for nitrogen, or soil too acid for the roots
to take what is there, or waterlogged roots — and finding out which is the game. **Botany** (and a magnifying lens up
close) makes the eye sharper: smaller signs show earlier, ripeness reads better (a master sees when produce is *at
its best*). An Edit Mode test keeps every observation free of causes and numbers.

## Instruments

Instruments are items (made at runtime or assets). **Measure** with one reads the spot. Each has a **quality** (0–1):
a crude one gives **classes** that can be **wrong** (each crude instrument has its own consistent bias, and its
readings wander), a good one numbers, a fine one precise numbers. Skill narrows the spread: Botany for most, Alchemy
for the soil analyses, Agriculture for the depth gauge and compaction tester. The same spot read in the same hour by
the same player reads the same (no re-rolling).

| Instrument | Quality | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| **Crude pH Probe** | 0.2 | *Strongly acidic … Neutral … Strongly alkaline* — roughly |
| **pH Probe** | 0.75 | pH to a tenth (a mid-quality probe to a half) |
| **Alchemical Soil Analysis Kit** | 0.5 | Nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, calcium, magnesium, sulfur as *very low … high* (a fine kit in ppm) |
| **Soil Moisture Meter** | 0.6 | Moisture % (a crude one: *Dry … Saturated*) |
| **Alchemical Thermometer** | 0.7 | Air and soil temperature |
| **Light Meter** | 0.6 | Light now (lux) and the day's sunlight so far (hours of full sun) |
| **Hygrometer** | 0.6 | Air humidity |
| **Soil Compaction Tester** | 0.5 | Resistance (MPa) or *loose … hard* |
| **Botanical Handbook** | — | What the books say of the plant here (the species' *Handbook* text, written by the designer: vague, partial, sometimes wrong — or nothing for plants the books do not know) |
| **Advanced Alchemical Analyzer** | 0.9 | Everything: pH, the twelve nutrients in ppm, organic matter, salinity, water held, drainage, compaction, magical essence, soil diseases, the soil type |
| **Depth Gauge** | 0.9 | How deep the hole is, how deep the seed lies (and a ruler in the soil view) |
| **Magnifying Lens** | 0.7 | Not measured with: carried, it makes **Look** up close show smaller pests and earlier signs |

**Write it down** puts the last reading into the player's **notebook**.

## The notebook

The player's own record: every reading they chose to write down and every note they typed, with the game date and
the field. It is theirs to fill (nothing is written for them) — what they sowed where, what they spread, what the probe
said before and after the limestone, which corner always wilts. Kept per player on their machine.

## The skills

Seven skills rise with the work they name (experience per action; a share of it also goes to the character's
Experience Manager). Each level makes the hands steadier and the eye sharper — they **never** give the answer.

| Skill | Rises with | Makes better |
|---|---|---|
| **Agriculture** | working the soil, digging, sowing, watering, spreading | Steadier digging depth, even spreading and watering, the depth gauge and compaction tester |
| **Botany** | looking, measuring, harvesting | Seeing symptoms and pests earlier, reading ripeness, most instruments |
| **Herbalism** | picking herbs, flowers and roots | Picking without bruising (quality), pulling roots whole |
| **Plant Medicine** | treating, picking off pests, pruning | Treatments that work better and harm less, clean cuts |
| **Alchemy** | soil analyses, alchemical materials | The analysis kit and analyzer |
| **Magical Cultivation** | growing magical plants | Their potency |
| **Seed Breeding** | saving seeds, hand pollination | Better seeds: mutations lean upwards |

The skills (and how fast they rise: *Max Level*, *Xp First Level*, *Xp Growth*, *Xp Multiplier*, *Character Xp Share*)
are set in the Agriculture Database. The **Skills** button of the field window shows them.
